## What The Diagnostic Does

Double-click:

```text
C:\D3\diagnose-crawl4ai.bat
```

It prompts for one Shopee product URL, then runs Crawl4AI in a separate temporary Chromium. It does **not**:

- connect to `127.0.0.1:9222`
- touch `chrome-cdp-d3-leon`
- steal or navigate Hermes tabs
- write `records.json`
- sync D1
- enter the 100-item production sweep

It writes:

```text
C:\D3\out\crawl4ai-diagnosis.json
C:\D3\out\crawl4ai-diagnosis.md
```

If the diagnosis verdict is abnormal and `.env` has `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` / `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID`, it sends one Telegram alert with verdict, reason, and URL. Successful diagnosis does not send Telegram.

## Interpretation Rules

Crawl4AI uses a fresh temporary Chromium by default. Shopee may block that browser even when Hermes production CDP works. Therefore:

- `Hermes CDP success + Crawl4AI fail` is expected and does **not** mean the dedicated Chrome is bad.
- `Hermes CDP fail + Crawl4AI fail` may indicate wider Shopee/IP/API/page-state trouble.
- `Hermes CDP fail + Crawl4AI success` is the most useful signal that the dedicated Chrome tab/session may be stale or dirty.

The diagnostic is an observation tool, not an anti-fingerprint or verification bypass.
